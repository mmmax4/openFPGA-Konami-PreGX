Konami pre-GX - Analogue Pocket openFPGA
By mmmax4, based on plasticbugs/Scott's Gaiapolis core.
Playable: Gaiapolis (ver EAF), Mystic Warriors (ver EAA).
Other family games are planned; Violent Storm is next.
Mystic: ninja names can remain after the intro portraits.
Test/Service start Unassigned; settings and game saves are independent.
Source, credits and status: https://github.com/mmmax4/openFPGA-Konami-PreGX
